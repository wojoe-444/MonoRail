# Find anything with Ctrl+K

Press **Ctrl+K** on any page, or click the search field in the top bar, and a
search box opens over the page. It looks through everything at once:

- **Pages**: every page in the side rail, such as Chat, Explore or Storage.
- **Catalog**: every kind of thing in the [MonoRail standard](../standard/README.md),
  such as tables, subagents, glossary terms or ingestion pipelines. Picking one
  opens [Explore](explore.md) at that kind, with its items listed.
- **Chats**: the conversations [Chat](agents/chat.md) lists, newest first.
  Archived chats are left out.
- **Settings**: every Settings page, card and option, the same as the search
  box in [Settings](setup/README.md#finding-your-way-around-settings).
- **Wiki**: every page of this wiki. With nothing typed it lists the guides.

On a Settings page, Ctrl+K opens on the Settings results first. Press Ctrl+K
again, Escape or click outside the box to close it.

To search inside your git repositories (code, files, commits and history),
use GitHub+'s own search: [search your code](workspace/search-code.md).

## Browse the catalog

Before you type, the box shows the last few things you opened and the
catalog's sections: MonoRail's own things, data assets, governance and so on.
Click a section (or move to it and press Enter) to list its kinds by
category; type to narrow them down. The section's name shows in the box;
click it, or press Backspace in the empty box, to go back.

**Open Explore** at the top of the sections jumps straight to
[Explore](explore.md), where you can see every item and field.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl+K | Open or close the search |
| Up / Down | Move through the results |
| Enter | Open the highlighted result |
| Tab / Shift+Tab | Switch between All, Pages, Catalog, Chats, Settings and Wiki |
| Backspace (empty box) | Leave a section, then go back to All |
| Escape | Close |

On a phone using [Remote access](setup/remote-access.md) there is no Ctrl
key: tap the search button in the top bar instead.

## What it keeps

Searching reads what MonoRail already knows; nothing is sent anywhere and
nothing new is stored, except a list of the last five results you opened, so
they show up first next time. It is kept with your
[profile](glossary.md#profile)'s other preferences. See
[private data stays local](private-data.md).
