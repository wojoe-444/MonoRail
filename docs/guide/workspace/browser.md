# Browser

**Tools > Browser** is a web browser inside MonoRail, with tabs, an address
bar, back, forward and reload. Type an address, or anything else to search
the web.

## Sign-ins

Sign in to a site once inside the Browser and you stay signed in, even after
MonoRail restarts. The Browser keeps its own sign-ins, apart from your real
Chrome:

- **It can't reuse your Chrome sign-ins.** Chrome locks its saved sign-ins so
  only Chrome can read them, and MonoRail doesn't try to get around that.
  Sign in once here instead.
- **Each [profile](../glossary.md#profile) has its own sign-ins.** The hidden
  New User test profile starts signed out every time.
- **Sign out of all sites** (the eraser button) clears the Browser's
  sign-ins, cookies, site data and cache for the current profile. Chrome is
  not affected.
- Like the rest of MonoRail's data, the sign-ins stay on your computer, as
  [private data stays local](../private-data.md) explains.

## Tabs and buttons

- **New tab**: the + button or Ctrl+T. Links that open a new tab open one
  here. Close a tab with its X, a middle click or Ctrl+W.
- Ctrl+L jumps to the address bar; Ctrl+R or F5 reloads.
- **Open in your browser** (the arrow button) opens the current page in your
  default browser, so you can use Chrome when a site needs your real Chrome
  sign-in.
- Your open tabs come back the next time you open the page.

## What it won't do

- Sites can't use your camera, microphone, location or notifications.
- It only works in MonoRail's own window on your PC, not in
  [Remote access](../setup/remote-access.md) on a phone.

For saved links instead of open tabs, use Bookmarks; for jotting notes while
you read, use [Notes](../../notes.md). Both sit next to the Browser in
[your workspace](README.md).
