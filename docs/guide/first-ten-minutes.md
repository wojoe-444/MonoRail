# Your first ten minutes

MonoRail works out of the box and does nothing until you ask
([nothing runs until you ask](private-data.md#nothing-runs-until-you-ask)).
Here is what is worth trying on day one.

## Take the tour

On your first launch, an interactive spotlight tour highlights the rail, Home,
Bots, Tools, the [wiki](README.md), Settings and the top bar. Step through with
**Next** / **Back** (or arrow keys), or press **Skip tour**. Re-run it any time
from **Take the tour** on Home or in **Settings > General**. Each
[profile](glossary.md#profile) sees it once.

## 1. Pick a look

Open **Settings > Appearance** to pick a color scheme, backdrop and light or
dark mode ([make it look right](setup/appearance.md)).

## 2. Open Chat

Open **Bots** (the Chat landing page) to see recent Claude Code and pi sessions
from [allowed folders](agents/chat.md#which-chats-are-listed) — by default, any
git [repository](glossary.md#repository) directly inside `~/Repos`. See
[Chat with your agents](agents/chat.md) for **Ask** vs **Agent** mode.

## 3. Connect your accounts

In **Settings > Connections**, add only the services you actually use (Claude,
GitHub, Atlassian, Google Cloud, pi, Docker and more). See
[connect your accounts](setup/connections.md) and how
[tokens stay encrypted](private-data.md#passwords-and-tokens-are-encrypted).

## 4. Check your spending

Open **Bots > Dashboard > Claude Stats** to see charts built from your local
agent logs ([keep an eye on spending](agents/spend.md)).

## 5. Open a repository

Open **Tools > GitHub+** to browse any local git repo with code, history,
branches and diffs ([browse your repos](workspace/browse-repos.md)).

## 6. Turn on backups

Open **Settings > Backups** and point it at a synced folder such as OneDrive
([backups and updates](setup/backups-and-updates.md)).
